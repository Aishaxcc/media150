Reading 1

Photomontage is a form of visual art that combines multiple photographs into a single composition. It has roots in movements like Dada and Surrealism, where artists experimented with cutting and rearranging images to create new meanings. Unlike general collage, photomontage focuses specifically on photographic elements, making it a powerful way to challenge realism and create layered visual narratives.

In contemporary art, photomontage also raises questions about copyright and image ownership. Artists must consider whether the images they use are in the public domain or licensed through Creative Commons. This highlights the balance between creativity and responsibility, showing that making art is not just about visuals, but also about ethical choices in how materials are used.
