In this assignment, I used Camera Raw to adjust exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows, and color balance. The RAW file allowed me to make stronger edits without losing as much quality, while the JPEG image had less flexibility. This process helped me understand the difference between JPEG and RAW files and how post-processing can improve the final image.
