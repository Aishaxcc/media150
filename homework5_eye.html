Homework 5: Vector Emotive Eye

For this assignment, I created a vector eye that expresses fear and intensity. I used a bold eye shape, sharp lashes, and a heavy brow to make the emotion feel more dramatic. I also made the image fill the artboard so the eye would be the main focus right away.

I built the design using simple shapes and strong color contrast. The black and red help make the image feel more intense, while the facial lines add to the overall expression and mood.
