Creative Statement

I’m Aisha M, also known as @Daavillain. My work is rooted in creativity, identity, and visual storytelling. I’m interested in how images, editing, and digital tools can transform everyday moments into something more expressive and intentional.

Through this course, I’ve been developing my skills in photography and Photoshop, learning how to manipulate depth, lighting, and composition. My goal is to create work that feels both personal and visually strong while building a clean and professional digital portfolio.
